Three steps

  1. Download and installPick your device above and open the file. On Windows the installer adds a desktop shortcut.
  2. Open the appA window opens with an empty link box. Light and dark themes are built in.
  3. Paste a link and press DownloadThe app checks the link and shows the video. It picks the best quality the video really has. Press Save to… to choose the folder. You can pause, and it continues where it stopped, even after the internet drops or you close the app.

The real best quality

If a video has 720p, you get 720p. If it has 4K, you get 4K. You can still pick a lower one.

Many gigabytes is fine

Your computer does the work, so there is no limit on length or file size.

Your links stay with you

No account. Nothing you paste is sent to us. The app only counts anonymous usage, and you can switch that off in Settings.
