Three steps

  1. Download and unzipPick your device above. Unzip the file anywhere you like.
  2. Open UltraVideoDownloaderDouble-click it. A window opens with an empty link box.
  3. Paste a link and press DownloadChoose the quality, up to 4K, or audio only as MP3. You see the speed and the time left. The file lands in Downloads, in the UltraVideoDownloader folder.
