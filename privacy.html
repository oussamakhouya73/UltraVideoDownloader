Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

The app

UltraVideoDownloader runs on your own device. The links you paste and the videos you save stay on your device. We do not run a server that receives your links, and we do not ask you to create an account.

Ads

This website and the app show ads from third-party advertising networks. These networks can use cookies and similar identifiers to show ads and to measure them. They may collect information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. We do not control what they collect. Please read the privacy policy of the network that serves the ad you see.

Ads are shown in a separate frame, so the ad code cannot read the links you paste.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can use an ad blocker. The app works the same without ads.

What the site itself collects

The website is a static page. It does not store personal information. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit, for security and operation.

Downloading content

Use the app only for videos you own or have permission to save. The terms of the site a video comes from still apply.
